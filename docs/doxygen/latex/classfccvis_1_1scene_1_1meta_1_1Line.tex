\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A line-\/segment graphical primitive. 

\doxylink{classfccvis_1_1scene_1_1meta_1_1Line}{Line} represents a straight segment between two three-\/dimensional positions.

A line has independently configurable color, width, and rendering style.
