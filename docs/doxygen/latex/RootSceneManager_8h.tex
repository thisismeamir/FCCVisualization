\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Management of materialized ROOT/\+\+TEve visualization scenes. 

Defines \doxylink{classfccvis_1_1backend_1_1root_1_1RootSceneManager}{fccvis::\+backend::\+root::\+\+Root\+Scene\+Manager}, which manages the lifetime and lookup of ROOT representations of abstract FCCVisualization scenes.

The manager maintains a clear separation between:


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item abstract scenes owned by the \doxylink{classfccvis_1_1session_1_1Session}{fccvis::\+session::\+\+Session}; and
\item their live ROOT/\+\+TEve materializations represented by \doxylink{classfccvis_1_1backend_1_1root_1_1RootScene}{fccvis::\+backend::\+root::\+\+Root\+Scene}.
\end{DoxyItemize}

Opening and closing a scene therefore affects only its ROOT-\/side representation. The abstract scene and session data remain independent of the ROOT scene lifetime.
