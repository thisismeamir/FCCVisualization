\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
ROOT/\+\+TEve representation of an abstract visualization scene. 

\doxylink{classfccvis_1_1backend_1_1root_1_1RootScene}{Root\+Scene} bridges the backend-\/independent \doxylink{classfccvis_1_1scene_1_1Scene}{fccvis::\+scene::\+\+Scene} representation and the corresponding ROOT/\+\+TEve graphical objects.

The class maintains a shared reference to the abstract Scene while owning or managing its associated TEve-\/side representation.

The abstract scene remains independent of ROOT and can therefore continue to exist separately from its materialized ROOT scene.
