Detailed Description

Management of materialized ROOT/TEve visualization scenes.

Defines fccvis::backend::root::RootSceneManager, which manages the lifetime and lookup of ROOT representations of abstract FCCVisualization scenes.

The manager maintains a clear separation between:

  • abstract scenes owned by the fccvis::session::Session; and
  • their live ROOT/TEve materializations represented by fccvis::backend::root::RootScene.

Opening and closing a scene therefore affects only its ROOT-side representation. The abstract scene and session data remain independent of the ROOT scene lifetime.
